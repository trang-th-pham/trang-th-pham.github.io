Novartis Biomedical Research · Summer 2024

Nanovial technology workflow

I was selected for a competitive summer research internship at Novartis, where I worked with Dr. Giang Nguyen and Dr. Alan Ho to develop an efficient workflow for Nanovial technology.

We used Nanovials to isolate antigen-specific, antibody-producing B cells. The workflow was designed to support therapeutic development by helping researchers identify and study B-cell responses more efficiently, shortening the path between laboratory discovery and patient treatment.
