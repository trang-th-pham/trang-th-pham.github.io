Bement Lab · University of Wisconsin–Madison

Wound-healing pathways

At Professor William Bement’s lab, I worked with PhD student Lila Hoachlander-Hobby to study the mechanisms behind single-cell wound healing using confocal microscopy.

My project focused on the role of the Arp2/3 complex in the Cdc42 signaling pathway during single-cell repair. Understanding these pathways may help inform improved treatments for genetic disorders, including muscular dystrophy, that impair healing.

This work is available as a preprint: A self-organized signaling hierarchy patterns the cortex during cell repair (ResearchGate).
