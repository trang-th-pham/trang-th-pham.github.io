University of Medicine & Pharmacy · Ho Chi Minh City

Hypertension treatment outcomes in Vietnam

During the summer after my first year, I worked with Professor Hai Yen Nguyen on a case study at a regional hospital in southern Vietnam.

Using statistical analysis in R, I evaluated the effectiveness of treatment options and quantified their uncertainty. We used the results to develop a proposal for an evidence-based treatment plan for the hospital.

The work contributed to a proceeding presented at the 4th ASEAN PharmNET Conference.
