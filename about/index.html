Research experience

At Professor William Bement’s lab, Trang studied the cellular pathways involved in wound repair. Her work used molecular cloning and confocal microscopy to investigate how the Arp2/3 complex contributes to single-cell wound healing, with potential relevance to disorders such as muscular dystrophy.

As a research intern at Novartis Biomedical Research, she helped develop a workflow using Nanovial technology to isolate antigen-specific, antibody-producing B cells. She also conducted clinical outcomes research at the University of Medicine and Pharmacy in Ho Chi Minh City, using statistical analysis in R to evaluate treatment effectiveness.

Beyond research

Trang has volunteered extensively with community food programs and has worked in clinical settings as a medical scribe. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking, cross-stitching, and making handmade gifts for friends and family.
