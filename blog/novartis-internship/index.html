Summer internship at Novartis

This summer, I had an amazing experience interning at Novartis Biomedical Research in Boston, where I worked with Nanovial technology for single-cell analysis of B cells and antibodies.

It was inspiring to see how this research could support personalized medicine and deepen our understanding of immune responses. Each day brought new challenges that strengthened my problem-solving and teamwork skills.

The supportive and collaborative community encouraged me to ask questions and share ideas. The internship became a defining part of my undergraduate experience and strengthened my commitment to healthcare.
