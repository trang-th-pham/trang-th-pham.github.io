ASCB Conference in San Diego

I had the opportunity to attend my first American Society for Cell Biology conference in San Diego. It was exciting to see scientists and industry experts present research across many areas of cell biology.

During the poster session, I presented my work on the Cdc42 signaling pathway in single cells. I also had thoughtful discussions with researchers from the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center whose protein-focused work connected directly with my project.

It was an inspiring and productive first conference experience.
