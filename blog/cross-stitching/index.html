Cross-stitching!

The holidays can get hectic, so I turn to cross-stitching to slow down and relax. There is something about it that helps me feel grounded and present, especially during such a busy time of year.

I started this hobby in sixth grade, and it has stayed with me ever since. I love creating small cross-stitch pieces as gifts for friends and family or as decorations for the house.

Each piece holds a special memory from the time I created it, and I hope it brings as much joy to others as it does to me.
